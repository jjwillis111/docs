SoFi: Executive Statements Aimed at Retail Investors

Summary

The public record supports the view that SoFi's leadership, and Anthony Noto in particular, actively cultivates a retail investor following. In the material reviewed, Noto does not frame his remarks as a formal recommendation, but he has told retail audiences the stock is undervalued, that it is "a good buying point" (Basis Points, 13:11 to 13:17), and that for anyone with a view longer than a few months it is "a great opportunity to buy it" (Future Investing, July 30, 2026, about 40:48 to 41:01).[1] Nothing surfaced is directed at Reddit or WallStreetBets by name. The pattern runs through four channels:

1.     Publicized insider buying. Noto makes repeated, relatively small open-market purchases during selloffs, which retail outlets and social media amplify as a buy signal. He has tied those purchases to his view that the stock is undervalued.[2]

2.     Retail-facing media. Noto appears on retail investing YouTube channels and podcasts and has appeared on CNBC with Jim Cramer. On one podcast in May 2026 he walked a retail audience through valuation math landing at a $25 to $35 stock, when it traded in the mid-teens; in an October 2026 interview he said he expects the stock "to kind of melt up."[3]

3.     Earnings-call messaging. Noto closed the Q2 2026 call by explaining why he buys the stock and predicting the market will eventually "connect the dots."[4]

4.     Product overlap. SoFi runs its own retail brokerage and has built products around giving members access to IPOs and investing.[5]

The most useful finding for an enforcement lens is a tension between narrative and filings. SoFi has emphasized that Noto has never sold a share, while Noto and CFO Chris Lapointe have monetized about $58 million of stock through prepaid variable forward contracts.[6] In the material reviewed, Lapointe's public appearances were mostly sell-side conferences, apart from a December 2023 retail shareholder Q&A announced for Twitter Spaces (Section 2).[7]

1. Publicized insider buying

Noto's open-market purchases are a recurring theme in retail coverage of SoFi.[8] They are small relative to his holdings of about 12 million shares,[9] but they have drawn retail attention and, on some occasions, price moves (see table). SoFi's August 2025 8-K states he bought 2,775,307 shares in the open market over the prior four years (the filing reads "2,775,307 million," an apparent typo).[10]

Noto's framing. In an interview with Future Investing, a retail YouTube channel (published Feb. 6, 2026), Noto said he viewed SoFi as undervalued and would be buying it at that price, but said he could not buy or sell the stock without significant tax implications until March 1, tying that to his prepaid variable forward (about 41:31 to 42:50). In the same interview he said a PEG-based valuation puts the stock "in the 30s" (about 7:26 to 7:38).[17] He bought about $1.0 million on March 2, the first trading day after that date.[18] On Basis Points he said that when he buys the stock, "I want the world to know how confident I am" (1:01:06 to 1:01:11).[19] On Tevis (Oct. 29) he said it is "really hard for me to come up with money to buy SoFi stock" without paying taxes on his other holdings (about 51:39 to 51:48).[20] In an April 2022 X post he wrote that he has "not sold a share even though it's my compensation, because I am confident in our company."[21]

Retail amplification. A Quiver Quantitative tracker of social media discussion summarized posts about the May 11 purchase as "a strong vote of confidence from leadership" (May 12, 2026).[22] The creator who interviewed Noto in February posted the May 8 purchase to roughly 378,000 views, and Barron's and MarketWatch pushed the March 2 purchase on X.[23] The material reviewed shows no instance of Noto personally announcing a purchase; the amplification comes from others picking up his reported purchases.

2. Podcasts, videos, and talks

Most significant item: Basis Points (May 22, 2026). Noto spent about an hour with two hosts who said their audience is largely retail investors; they asked him about his open-market purchases and the stock price within the first four minutes. In the transcript, he says the stock's two-times tangible book multiple "should not go below two times," asks why SoFi should not trade above JPMorgan (which he says trades at three times tangible book), and says a PEG-based approach gets to about $25 and five times tangible book to over $35, so that "easily you can get to a justified $25 to $35, which is why I've been buying the stock." He also said he does not see why SoFi cannot be a trillion-dollar company.[24] He also said he had bought about $2 million of stock that year (13:17; the auto-generated transcript is garbled at that word),[25] which matches his Form 4 purchases from March through May 11 (about $2.0 million).[26] He referred to a $15 stock at about 1:01:16 to 1:01:22, and his May 8 and 11 purchases were at about $15.73 and $16.00.[27] This is the closest thing in the material reviewed to a CEO giving a retail audience a price target.

Most recent item: Tevis (Oct. 29, 2026). Recorded after Q3 results, the interview used questions pulled from an X poll of the host's audience. Noto said he expects the stock "to kind of melt up" until a negative thesis emerges (56:38 to 56:46), arguing that a short seller at $16, about two times tangible book, is betting on an earnings miss (about 55:54). He said he wants "everyone to get wealthy as hell off of owning our stock" (51:23). Asked whether SoFi should stay independent rather than be acquired by a larger bank, he said independence is important to its culture and that he is "staying here until they kick me out or I die" (42:01 to 44:19). He also said he watches social media (1:03:20).[28]

Retail shareholder Q&A (Dec. 4, 2023). Noto and Lapointe answered questions submitted and upvoted by shareholders through Say Technologies, a retail investor platform.[39] SoFi promoted the event on its IR account on X and announced it for Twitter Spaces.[40] In the transcript, Noto addresses the "Twitter universe" and says he reads their tweets.[41]

Jim Cramer. Noto spoke with Cramer on April 29, 2026, after Q1 results.[42] Benzinga reported in June 2021 that the two have been friends since 1995, and quoted Cramer as "a buyer of Anthony Noto."[43] In July 2025 Cramer said, "I'm staying with Noto."[44]

Social media and audience. SoFi's releases name X and LinkedIn as investor communication channels; whether that covers Noto's personal account is worth checking against SoFi's Regulation FD disclosures.[45] Third-party estimates of non-institutional ownership range from about 46% (the inverse of Muddy Waters' 53.7% institutional figure) to 56% (Simply Wall St, Oct. 2024 data) to about 59% (TipRanks, current, which lumps in public companies), so methodologies conflict.[46] Muddy Waters argued SoFi's low institutional ownership "seems to be a choice."[47]

3. Earnings-call messaging

Noto also explains why he buys the stock on earnings calls. Closing the Q2 2026 call, he said he is often asked why he buys the stock: SoFi will reach a 20% to 30% return on tangible common equity, and it is "just a matter of when, not if" the market connects the dots.[48] A retail investor account on X quoted the line approvingly the same day, praising a CEO who keeps buying the stock himself.[49] On the same call he framed SoFi's goal as giving everyday investors access to opportunities they have not historically had, citing its SpaceX IPO offering and an options-income ETF built on the 50 most widely held stocks in SoFi self-directed brokerage accounts.[50]

4. "Never sold a share" vs. prepaid variable forwards

A prepaid variable forward (PVF) is not a sale in form, but it delivers cash upfront and, between the floor and cap prices, fixes the value of the shares the holder will deliver at maturity.[51]

The same August 2025 8-K that disclosed the $24.1 million PVF told investors Noto "has not sold any Company Common Stock since joining the Company in early 2018."[55] Muddy Waters put combined Noto and Lapointe PVF proceeds at $58.3 million and called Noto's stock purchases "token."[56] The three Form 4 filings above total $58,342,880 ($22,463,200 plus $24,107,850 plus $11,771,830), which matches the Muddy Waters figure. The December 2024 forward (maturing Feb. 7, 2028) predates the August 2025 8-K, and only its Form 4 was located.[57] SoFi called the short report "factually inaccurate and misleading" and said it believes it is "designed to deceive investors," but its press release did not respond to any specific allegation, and Fortune reported that a person close to the company said SoFi chose not to address the allegations publicly.[58] Muddy Waters states that it is short SOFI, so its characterizations should be weighed accordingly.[59] Separately, SoFi priced a stock offering at $27.50 per share in December 2025,[60] and shares later fell more than 40% below that price.[61]

5. Context: retail products, the SPAC, and the CFO

SoFi went public in June 2021 through a SPAC led by Chamath Palihapitiya.[62] In March 2021, before the merger closed, SoFi announced it would give members with at least $3,000 in account value early access to IPO shares.[63] This is context, not evidence of stock promotion, but because SoFi runs a retail brokerage, the line between promoting its products and its stock may be worth examining.

In the material reviewed, Lapointe's appearances apart from the 2023 Q&A were sell-side fireside chats (for example, Stephens in Nov. 2023) and earnings calls.[64] The items touching him are the November 2025 PVF and a Muddy Waters allegation, which SoFi disputes, that he appears to have misled investors by describing a $312 million loan transfer as a sale.[65]

6. Conclusion

Noto and SoFi cultivate a retail following through publicized insider buying, retail-facing media, and earnings-call messaging, and Noto has told retail audiences the stock is undervalued and a good buying point.[66] Nothing surfaced is directed at Reddit or WallStreetBets by name.

The tension for an enforcement lens is between narrative and filings. SoFi has said Noto has never sold a share, while Noto and CFO Chris Lapointe have monetized about $58 million of stock through prepaid variable forward contracts.[67] The 2025 proxy statement does not disclose the forwards, although its policy exempts approved forward-based monetization contracts,[68] so whether the 8-K and Form 4 disclosures were sufficient is something to watch going forward.
