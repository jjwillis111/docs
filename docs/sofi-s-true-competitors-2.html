SoFi's True Competitors

Bottom line

The population of true matches is small today but growing fast. Only one public company closely matches SoFi's model of a digital-first consumer lender that owns its own bank and funds loans with its own deposits: Happen (formerly LendingClub), whose bank is now Happen Bank.[1] Two others hold bank charters and lend to consumers but differ materially in scale or product: Varo (private, small lending book) and Block (whose industrial bank now originates short-term Cash App Borrow loans).[2]

Two student lenders, Nelnet and Sallie Mae, also lend to consumers out of their own banks, though neither is a fintech app.[3] The bigger story is the pipeline. Chime agreed in September 2026 to buy Stride Bank.[4] Upgrade and Avant, both direct personal loan competitors, applied for their own banks since mid-September 2026.[5] Upstart received conditional OCC approval for a national bank in July 2026, Nubank received conditional approval for a U.S. bank in January 2026, and Affirm has an industrial bank application pending.[6] Enova and OppFi were on that path but backed out: Enova withdrew its bank acquisition applications in September 2026, and OppFi withdrew its applications on Nov. 3, 2026.[7]

The broader "close but not on point" group splits two ways:

1.     Fintechs without a bank: Upstart, Upgrade, Avant, Affirm, Klarna, and Mission Lane (all with bank applications in process or conditionally approved), Best Egg (now owned by Barclays), Chime (acquiring a bank), Robinhood, Enova, and OppFi.[8]

2.     Banks without the fintech model: large digital or card-led banks such as Ally, Synchrony, and Capital One (which now owns Discover).[9]

SoFi itself does not name competitors in its 10-K, and its compensation peer group is built on size, not business model.[10] Public coverage divides in a similar way: fintech-focused coverage groups SoFi with Chime, Robinhood, Affirm, and Block, while Morningstar lists Ally and Synchrony and some analysts frame SoFi against traditional banks and card issuers.[11]

The screen

A "true competitor" here means a company that meets all three of these tests:

1.     Consumer lending is a core business, centered on unsecured personal loans or similar products, rather than mortgages (which rules out Rocket) or business lending.

2.     It owns an FDIC-insured bank and funds loans with its own deposits, rather than renting a charter from a partner bank. SoFi has operated a national bank since it acquired Golden Pacific Bancorp in February 2022,[12] and Noto says smaller personal loan competitors have a higher cost of funding than SoFi.[13]

3.     It is digital-first, acquiring customers through an app rather than branches.

The second test is what shrinks the population. Most consumer fintechs still rely on partner banks (Avant and Klarna, for example, use WebBank),[14] although a wave of charter applications in 2025 and 2026 is changing that.[15]

Closest competitors: digital consumer lenders that own a bank

Happen (formerly LendingClub) is the only company that clearly passes all three tests today.[16] Varo and Block pass the screen on paper but are weaker matches on product and scale.[17]

How SoFi describes its own competition

SoFi's filings define competitors by category and name no companies.[52]

10-K. The competition section lists seven arenas: personal, student, card, and residential lenders; deposits, where it names other banks, challenger banks, and technology and retail companies; social and commerce payment platforms; brokerage; digital asset trading; financial content; and enterprise technology. For lending, it describes a fragmented field of banks, credit unions, specialty finance lenders, and technology-enabled lenders.[53]

Compensation peer group. The closest thing to a SoFi-chosen list is the peer group its compensation committee approved in July 2024, as described in the 2026 proxy.[54] It includes Affirm, Block, Robinhood, Capital One, Charles Schwab, PayPal, SLM, OneMain, Interactive Brokers, Ameriprise, Equifax, Fair Isaac, First Horizon, F.N.B., Jack Henry & Associates, Pinnacle Financial Partners, Shift4, Toast, Etsy, Pinterest, and Zillow.[55] The committee selects peers on revenue, market capitalization, and scope of business, so the list mixes payments, brokerage, banks, and consumer internet companies.[56]

Two points stand out. Of the companies in the tables above, Block, Affirm, Robinhood, PayPal, Capital One, and SLM (the parent of Sallie Mae Bank) appear, while LendingClub, Varo, and Nelnet are absent.[57] Upstart and LendingTree were in SoFi's original 2021 peer group, but SoFi later removed Upstart as no longer an appropriate peer and LendingTree for falling outside the market-cap range.[58]

Pattern. Fintech coverage most often pairs SoFi with Chime, Robinhood, Affirm, and Block.[65] Morningstar points to Ally and Synchrony.[66] In the public coverage reviewed, only the short seller names LendingClub as a peer,[67] even though LendingClub (now Happen) is the closest business-model match.[68]

Conclusion

Only one public company, Happen (formerly LendingClub), closely matches SoFi's model of a digital-first consumer lender that funds loans with deposits at its own bank.[69] Varo and Block each match only part of it: Varo has a small lending book, and Block's industrial bank originates short-term Cash App Borrow loans.[70]

Analysts do not agree on who SoFi's competitors are. Fintech coverage groups SoFi with Chime, Robinhood, Affirm, and Block, while Morningstar and others frame it against Ally, Synchrony, and traditional lenders.[71] Apart from a short seller's report, none of the public coverage reviewed names LendingClub, the closest business-model match.[72]

The landscape is still open. The OCC has received more than 40 de novo charter applications since January 2025.[73] Avant and Upgrade applied for their own banks in September and October 2026, Chime agreed to buy Stride Bank, and Upstart is conditionally approved and would become a close public match if it completes the process.[74] Not every attempt succeeds: Enova and OppFi withdrew theirs.[75] This is something to watch going forward, and the list above reflects public reporting through November 10, 2026.
